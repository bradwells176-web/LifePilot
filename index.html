What kind of life are you trying to build?
Don't worry about sounding impressive. Think about what would make your actual life better.
Ideas to get your brain moving
“Have more free time.”
“Finish my truck project.”
“Take my kids camping.”
“Stop living paycheck to paycheck.”
“Get my garage under control.”
“Learn something I've always been curious about.”
“Travel somewhere I've never been.”
“Be healthier without making my whole life about exercise.”
What are some goals you'd actually enjoy accomplishing?
You don't need ten goals. One meaningful goal is enough.
Try thinking smaller and more specifically
Instead of “save money” → “save my first $500 emergency fund.”
Instead of “get fit” → “walk 20 minutes three times this week.”
Instead of “clean the house” → “finally clean out the garage shelves.”
Instead of “learn guitar” → “learn three songs I can actually play.”
What should I know about your normal life?
Anything that affects what you can realistically accomplish.
Examples
“I work Monday through Friday, 8–5.”
“I work nights.”
“I have kids every other week.”
“Saturday mornings are usually free.”
“I hate mornings.”
“I have the most energy after lunch.”
